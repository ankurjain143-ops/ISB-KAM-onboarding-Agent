# FirstGear Knowledge Base – README & Source Map

**Purpose:** Learning content for the FirstGear KAM induction website (Days 1–15, per §8.1 of the Team 8 Review-2 report) and the grounding corpus for the FirstGear chatbot.

**Prepared by:** Team 8, ALP – for prototype/demonstration use only.

## Source labels used in every file

Every section carries one of these tags so the chatbot can cite it and reviewers can see what is real vs. illustrative:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.X]** | Summarised from the PVNA Group HR Employee Handbook (Doc No. HR-M-01, Rev 02, 03 April 2026). Page numbers refer to the PDF. |
| **[Source: padminivna.com]** | Taken from the public Padmini VNA website (About Us, Technologies, Customers pages). |
| **[Industry standard]** | Generic automotive-industry practice (e.g. AIAG APQP/PPAP, 8D). Not company-specific. |
| **[Illustrative – Team 8]** | Developed by Team 8 to fill gaps for the prototype. Plausible but NOT Padmini VNA's actual process, numbers or customers. Must be validated with the sponsor before any real use. |

## Confidentiality note

The HR Manual states it is for internal use and must not be reproduced without the Group CHRO's permission. Content here is summarised, not reproduced in full. Keep the prototype link private (or obtain HR permission) before sharing it outside the team and faculty panel. Personal phone numbers from the manual have been deliberately left out.

## Chatbot citation rule

When answering, cite the file title and section, e.g. *"Source: HR Policies – Travel Policy (PVNA HR Manual, p.66–68)"*. If a fact comes from an **[Illustrative – Team 8]** section, the chatbot should say it is illustrative sample content.
