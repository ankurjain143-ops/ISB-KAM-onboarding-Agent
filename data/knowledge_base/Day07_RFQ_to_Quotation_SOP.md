# Day 7 – RFQ to Quotation: Standard Operating Procedure

**Pillar:** Processes (RFQ) | **Owner:** Sales + Engineering
**Learning goal:** The KAM can take a customer RFQ from receipt to a submitted, approved quotation – knowing each step, owner and document.

> This SOP is **[Illustrative – Team 8]**, based on common Tier-1 auto-component practice.

---

## 1. What is an RFQ?
An RFQ (Request for Quotation) is a customer's formal request for PVNA to quote price, tooling, timing and technical compliance for a part or system – usually for a new vehicle programme or a re-sourcing.

## 2. Process overview

| Step | Activity | Owner | Output | Target time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive RFQ, log it in CRM, acknowledge the customer | KAM | RFQ register entry, acknowledgement mail | Same day |
| 2 | Check RFQ completeness (drawings, specs, volumes, SOP date, deadline, commercial terms) | KAM | Clarification list to customer | 1 day |
| 3 | Go / No-Go decision (strategic fit, capacity, capability, margin potential) | KAM Head + Engineering | Go/No-Go note | 2 days |
| 4 | Kick-off meeting with cross-functional team | KAM | RFQ kick-off minutes, task owners | 2 days |
| 5 | Technical feasibility and deviations list | Engineering / NPD | Feasibility commitment, deviations | 3–5 days |
| 6 | Process plan, capacity and investment (tooling, equipment) | Plant / Industrial Engineering | Process and capex inputs | 3–5 days |
| 7 | Material and bought-out quotes | SCM / Purchase | BOM costs | 3–5 days |
| 8 | Cost build-up and price proposal | Finance – Costing | Cost sheet, proposed price (see Day 8) | 2 days |
| 9 | Internal quote review and approval as per approval matrix | KAM Head / Business Head / CFO / CEO | Approved quotation | 1–2 days |
| 10 | Submit quotation in customer's format / portal | KAM | Submitted quote, record in CRM | Before deadline |
| 11 | Follow-up, negotiation rounds, revised quotes (each re-approved) | KAM | Negotiation log | As needed |
| 12 | Nomination / LOI received, or lost-bid analysis | KAM | Handover to Programme (Day 9) or lessons learned | – |

## 3. RFQ completeness checklist
- Part drawings and 3D data (latest revision)
- Technical specification and test requirements
- Annual volumes and lifetime volumes, by year
- SOP (start of production) date and programme timeline
- Quote submission deadline and format
- Commercial terms: Incoterms, payment terms, packaging, price validity, currency
- Tooling ownership and payment terms
- Customer-specific quality requirements
- Target price (if shared)

## 4. KAM rules during the RFQ
- **Never** share a price, even an "indicative ballpark", before internal approval.
- **Never** accept a customer's target price verbally – take it back to Costing.
- Keep the customer's drawings and data confidential; share them internally only with the RFQ team [Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.27 – client confidential information].
- Do not discuss the RFQ with competitors in any form [Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.28 – fair competition].
- Document every customer clarification and change in CRM.

## 5. Common mistakes
- Missing the submission deadline because feasibility started late.
- Quoting on an old drawing revision.
- Forgetting tooling, testing, packaging or logistics costs.
- Promising an SOP date before Engineering and Plant confirm.
