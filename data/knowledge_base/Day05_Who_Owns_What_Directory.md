## 4. Routing rule of thumb
[Illustrative – Team 8]

1. Identify the **type** of request (technical, commercial, delivery, quality, legal).
2. Go to the **primary owner** in the table.
3. Tell the customer **when** you will respond, not **what** the answer is, until the owner confirms.
4. If there's no answer within the agreed time, escalate as per the Day 6 escalation map.
