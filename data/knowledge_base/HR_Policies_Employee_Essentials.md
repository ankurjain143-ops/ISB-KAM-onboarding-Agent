## 1. Leave Policy
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.60–62]

| Leave type | Entitlement | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 7 days per calendar year (Jan–Dec) | Credited at the start of the year, pro-rata for mid-year joiners. Unused CL lapses at year-end. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 7 days per calendar year | Same crediting as CL. Unused SL accumulates up to 21 days. |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 21 days per year, credited monthly | Based on days present. Accumulates up to 45 days; excess on 31 Dec is encashed at gross salary. |
| Short Leave | 2 hours, twice a month | Department Head may refuse due to work exigencies. |
| Compensatory Off | Only when HOD/Vertical Head pre-approves work on a holiday/weekly off | 4.5 hrs = ½ day; 7 hrs = full day. Lapses if not used within 90 days. Cannot be encashed. Also for official training or travel on a holiday, with proof. |
| Maternity Leave | Up to 26 weeks, for up to 2 children | As per the Maternity Benefit Act. |
| Paternity Leave | 5 days, for up to 2 children | Also applies to legally adopted children. |
| Bereavement Leave | Up to 5 days per calendar year | For death of spouse, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, parents-in-law. Doesn't carry over. |

**General rules:**
- Apply for leave through HRMS for department-head approval. If not applied in advance, apply on the day you resume, or you'll be marked absent and pay deducted.
- Maximum 15 days at a time; beyond that, top management's discretion applies.
- Leave can be combined with holidays; Sundays, holiday Saturdays and holidays within the leave period aren't counted.
- Leave beyond entitlement = Leave Without Pay.
- A fitness certificate is needed after infectious illnesses (e.g. measles, chicken pox, conjunctivitis).
- No leave during the notice period; if taken, the notice period is extended by the same number of days.
- Public holidays are notified at the start of each year.

## 2. Travel Policy
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.66–69]

**General rules (important for KAMs, who travel frequently):**
- All travel needs prior approval of the Reporting Manager and HOD, and must match a business need.
- After travel, submit the Travel Expense Form with the **Travel Outcome Report (Annex-T1)**, approved by Reporting Manager and Function Head, with actual bills.
- Hand in approved claims to Accounts **within 5 days** of return; later claims need a reason and Function Head approval.
- Not reimbursed: alcohol, cigarettes, tips, charity or donations. Meal bills can't include liquor.
- Falsifying bills is serious misconduct.
- No personal work on official tours; no leave during official travel unless approved.
- Maximum tour length: 7 days at a stretch, including weekends.
- Travel advance can be claimed for trips over 5 days, with HOD and CHRO approval.
- Laundry reimbursed if the stay is over one week. If you make your own boarding/lodging arrangements, 25% allowance is paid.
- All limits exclude GST and are maximums – choose cheaper decent options where available.

**Local travel:** Uber for Business is the default; where Uber isn't available, best option on actuals. Prior reporting-manager approval needed. No Travel Outcome Report needed. Meal allowance at 50% of eligibility if local travel exceeds 6 hours.

## 4. Work From Home Policy
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.35–37]
- Eligibility is assessed by the HOD. Roles needing face-to-face customer/supplier interaction are generally **not suited** to WFH – relevant for KAMs.
- Requirements: company laptop, broadband of 10 Mbps or more, trained on VPN and video-conferencing tools.
- Sign in to the company chat tool before 9 am and stay online through working hours. Submit a daily task sheet in HRMS for manager approval; if rejected, the day may be marked as leave/absent.
- Managers can call you to office even on a WFH day. Travelling isn't counted as WFH; Sundays/holidays don't generate C-off.

## 5. Internet, Email, Laptop & Mobile
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.39–41, 71]
- Internet and email are for business use only; access is logged and monitored. No personal shopping, games, online contests, chain letters or unauthorised downloads.
- Add the company disclaimer after your signature on external emails.
- The company may inspect any system at any time; there's no expectation of privacy on company devices.
- No personal pen drives without IT permission and virus scan.
- Laptops/data cards are nominated by the HOD, typically for field roles such as sales. **Report loss or damage within 24 hours** to IT/Admin/HR; if negligence is found, a CEO–HR–Finance committee decides the penalty.
- Mobile: HOD decides eligibility; post-paid company CUG connection only, no prepaid reimbursement.

- Annual health check-up after one year of service: GM & above ₹12,000; staff above 40 years ₹6,000; workers above 40 not covered by ESI ₹4,500. Check-up day counts as On Duty; claim via Darwinbox.

## 9. Salary Advance & Contingency Loan
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.73–74]
For marriage (self/children), family medical emergencies or other pressing reasons. Under 2 years' service: not eligible. 2–5 years: up to 2 months' gross salary, repay within 12 months. Over 5 years: up to 4 months' gross, repay within 24 months. Request via Reporting Manager to Corporate Function Head / Plant Head. Interest-free (taxable as perquisite). Outstanding amount is repayable immediately on resignation.

## 10. Employee Referral (Mera Sathi)
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.13–14]
Submit referral CVs through HRMS. Bonus paid after the referred employee completes 6 months and is confirmed: Band 1 ₹20,000; Band 2 ₹15,000; Band 3 ₹10,000; Band 4 ₹8,000; Band 5 ₹5,000; Band 6 ₹3,000 (by the band the referral joins at). ## 11. Employee Suggestion Scheme
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.76–77]
Drop signed suggestions in the suggestion boxes. A Suggestion Evaluation Committee (3 HODs, rotating yearly) awards the best suggestions quarterly, scored on originality (20), savings/productivity (50), safety and work environment (15) and team building (15). Complaints, policy matters and repeat suggestions aren't accepted.

## 12. Grievance Redressal
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.79–80]
First try informal discussion with your supervisor. If unresolved, or if you're uncomfortable, go to senior management or HR. Records of appeals are not kept in your personnel file and there is no penalty for using the process. A Grievance Redressal Committee led by the Head of HR investigates; outcomes are communicated in writing.

## 13. POSH – Prevention of Sexual Harassment
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.48–58]
PVNA has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, in line with the POSH Act 2013. The policy covers office, plant, client sites, official travel and company events, and applies to conduct by clients, vendors and contractors too. Complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC) in writing, or by email to IC members, within 3 months of the incident. The IC aims to finish its inquiry within 90 days, and retaliation is prohibited. The current IC members are listed in Schedule II of the HR Manual – ask HR for contact details.

## 14. Vigil Mechanism (Whistle-blower)
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.82–87]
Report fraud, bribery, conflict of interest, false expense claims, misuse of assets, leaks of sensitive information, anti-competitive behaviour, safety non-compliance, harassment, child labour, discrimination or human-rights violations. Reports go to the Ethics Committee (CFO, CHRO, COO). Anonymity is allowed except for harassment, child labour, discrimination and human-rights cases. The Audit Committee Chairman can be reached at info@padminivna.com if you face retaliation. False complaints can lead to action.

## 15. Separation
[Source: PVNA HR Manual, p.89–93]
- Notice period: DGM & above 90 days; Assistant Manager to AGM 60 days; Senior Engineer/Executive & below 30 days.
- Resign in writing to your reporting manager, copying HR; complete the No-Dues checklist and exit interview. Full & final settlement takes at least 45 days after the last working day.
- Superannuation at 60.
- On separation, access to confidential information is revoked, and all company property (laptop, phone, ID, documents) must be returned. Confidentiality and non-solicitation obligations continue after you leave.
