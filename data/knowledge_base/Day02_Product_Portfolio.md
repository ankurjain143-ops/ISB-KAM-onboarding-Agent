# Day 2 – Product Portfolio: ICE, Hybrid, EV, Agnostic & Sensors

**Pillar:** Products | **Owner:** Product / Engineering
**Learning goal:** The KAM can name PVNA's product families, place each in the ICE / Hybrid / EV transition, and match products to vehicle segments.

## 1. How the portfolio is organised
[Source: padminivna.com – Technologies]

PVNA organises its technologies two ways:
- **By segment:** Two & Three Wheelers; M&HCV and LCV (commercial vehicles); Passenger Vehicles; Tractor & Off-Road.
- **By category:** ICE, Hybrid, EV, Agnostic (powertrain-independent) and Sensors.

A KAM should be able to answer both "what do we make for this vehicle type?" and "what do we make for this powertrain?"

## 2. ICE (Internal Combustion Engine) products
[Source: padminivna.com – ICE category]

EGR Modules; Smart EGR Valve; Electronic Throttle Valve / Electronic Throttle Control Valve; Exhaust Throttle Valve; Exhaust Brake Valve; Vacuum Pump; Canister Purge Valve (4W); Fuel Cap; Fuel Delivery Module; Gear Oil Pump; DC and BLDC Pumps; BLDC Actuators; Actuators (general purpose); Smart Actuator; Torque Motor; Electronic Expansion Valve; Coolant Temperature Sensor.

**Why it matters** [Illustrative – Team 8, based on general industry knowledge]: PVNA's heritage is emission control. EGR (Exhaust Gas Recirculation) routes part of the exhaust back into the intake to lower combustion temperature and NOx emissions – a core requirement for BS-VI and Euro-norm compliance. Throttle and exhaust valves control air and exhaust flow electronically. Canister purge and fuel-system valves control fuel vapour (evaporative emissions).

## 3. Hybrid products
[Source: padminivna.com – Hybrid category]

Fuel Tank Isolation Valve; Vapor Blocking Valve; Anti Corking Valve; Refueling Vent Valve; Fuel Cap; Canister Purge Valve (4W); Fuel Delivery Module; Grill Shutter Actuator; eWP (electric water pump) – Higher Power and Lower Power; CFCV – Linear.

**Why it matters** [Illustrative – Team 8]: In hybrids the engine runs less often, so the fuel tank must be sealed and vapour managed differently – hence isolation and vapour-blocking valves. Active grill shutters reduce aerodynamic drag and help thermal management.

## 4. EV products
[Source: padminivna.com – EV category]

IPM Motor; IPM Edrive; Drive Train; MCU/VCU (Motor / Vehicle Control Unit); DC-DC Converter; eWP Higher Power and Lower Power; Rotary Coolant Control Valve; Charge Port Actuator; AFT Motor; Disc Drum Brake.

**Why it matters** [Illustrative – Team 8]: EVs need electric drive (motor + controller), power conversion and thermal management for battery and motor. Several products (eWP, coolant valves) carry over from ICE/hybrid know-how, which is a strong story for customers transitioning their portfolios.

## 5. Agnostic and Sensors
[Source: padminivna.com; PVNA HR Manual, p.5]

- **Agnostic** products work across powertrains (e.g. actuators, pumps, thermal-management components).
- **Sensors:** Coolant Temperature Sensor on the website; the group's sensor heritage includes wheel-speed sensors (WSS) and accelerator pedal sensors (APS) from the 2008–2013 TT Electronics JV.
- The HR Manual also lists seating system and fuel system products within the wider group portfolio.

## 7. How to talk about products with a customer
[Illustrative – Team 8]

- Lead with the customer's problem (emission norm, efficiency, electrification timeline), then the product.
- Never quote specifications, prices or lead times from memory – confirm with Product/Engineering and follow the RFQ process (Day 7).
- Technical drawings and customer specifications are confidential (Code of Conduct, Day 1).
