# Day 6 – KAM Charter, KPIs, Approval Authority & Escalation Map

**Pillar:** Governance (KAM charter) | **Owner:** Reporting Boss (Harit Bhasin)
**Learning goal:** The KAM knows exactly what they own, how they are measured, what they may and may not approve, and when and how to escalate.

> This entire file is **[Illustrative – Team 8]** – a realistic KAM governance model for a Tier-1 auto-component supplier. Thresholds must be validated with the sponsor.

---

## 1. KAM Charter

**Purpose:** The KAM is PVNA's single point of accountability for the commercial relationship with assigned customer accounts – growing the business profitably while protecting customer satisfaction.

**The KAM owns:**
- Account plan and relationship map for each assigned customer
- RFQ intake, coordination and quotation submission (within approval authority)
- Customer communication on programmes, schedules, quality and commercial matters
- Tracking of open commitments, actions and escalations
- Order booking, forecast updates and collections follow-up
- Monthly account review with the Reporting Boss

**The KAM does NOT own (but coordinates):** technical feasibility (Engineering), cost estimation (Finance), quality investigations (Quality), dispatch (SCM/Plant), legal terms (Legal).

**New-KAM restriction:** During Days 1–15, a new KAM makes **no pricing, discount, delivery or other commitments** to a customer. Guided pricing exposure starts from Day 22, and only after the Day-15 assessment (Green band) and Day-21 scenario certification.

## 2. KPIs

| KPI | Target (illustrative) |
|---|---|
| RFQ response turnaround | Quotation submitted within customer deadline; internal target ≤10 working days |
| RFQ-to-nomination conversion | ≥25% by value |
| Order booking vs. annual plan | ≥100% |
| Customer complaint response | Acknowledged same day; containment within 24 hours |
| Collections / overdue receivables | Overdue ≤5% of account receivables |
| Customer satisfaction (CSAT / supplier rating) | Maintain or improve customer's supplier rating |
| Price-revision recovery | Raw-material increases recovered per contract formula |

## 4. Escalation map

| Level | When | Who | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Routine query or delay | Function owner (Day 5 map) | Same day |
| L2 | No response in 2 working days, or customer is dissatisfied | Function head + KAM Head | Within 24 hours of trigger |
| L3 | Line-stop risk at customer, safety/regulatory issue, major commercial dispute, or customer escalates to senior level | Business Head + Head of Quality/Plant | Immediately (same day) |
| L4 | Risk of losing the account or a legal/regulatory exposure | CEO | Immediately |

**Golden rules:** Escalate early, with facts (what, since when, impact, what you've tried, what you need). Never let a customer hear about a problem from someone other than their KAM.

## 5. Governance calendar

| Cadence | Forum | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Account action review | KAM, Mentor (during induction), function SPOCs |
| Monthly | Account performance review (sales, receivables, quality, RFQ pipeline) | KAM, KAM Head |
| Quarterly | Customer business review (QBR) | KAM, KAM Head, customer purchase/quality leads |
| Annually | Account plan and price negotiation | KAM, KAM Head, Finance, Business Head |
