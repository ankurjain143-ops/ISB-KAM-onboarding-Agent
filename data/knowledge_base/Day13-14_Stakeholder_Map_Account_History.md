## 3. Past commitments still valid
- Annual price-reduction discussions happen once a year in January; no ad-hoc reductions.
- PVNA keeps two weeks of safety stock at the Manesar warehouse for throttle valves.
- Any process change on Aravalli parts requires 90 days' advance notice (PCN).

## 4. Lessons learned from the prior KAM (Shree Pallavi)
1. **Aravalli values early warning.** The 2025 delivery issue damaged trust mainly because they heard late. Tell them early, with a plan.
2. **Bring engineering to purchase meetings.** Technical credibility won the 2019 nomination, not price.
3. **Trade, don't concede.** The 2024 price discussion worked because the reduction was linked to new business.
4. **Build the new Head of Purchase relationship with data** – supplier scorecard, cost-down ideas, localisation proposals.
5. **Close 8Ds on time.** Supplier Quality tracks closure time as closely as PPM.
